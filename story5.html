ABOUT THE BOOK

Dedication

This book was written to tell those who feel like the world is falling on them. And like the Lone did in the story he never gave up and did not to force anyone to accept him. So you can be happy because your happiness is all you deserve.

Also, you may feel left out in the world but know one thing is for sure, that no matter the place or environment you may find yourself, you will definitely lead and that is for sure because GOD HAS WRITTEN IT and so it will remain.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

DAVID O.I EMEKA

He is a boy from an average family with big dreams but had little resources to carry out his projects. Despite that he never gave up — he kept strong and continued his journey of life.

He passed through DE-PAUL CATHOLIC NURSERY AND PRIMARY SCHOOL (crèche to primary five), then COMMUNITY JUNIOR GRAMMAR SCHOOL for junior and MILLENNIUM SENIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL for senior, where he wrote his WASSCE.

He got admission into YABA COLLEGE OF TECHNOLOGY (YABATECH) studying COMPUTER ENGINEERING for OND, then UNIVERSITY OF LAGOS (UNILAG) for BSc, then furthered his education outside the country.

He joined the Boys Scouts Association when he was in JSS3.
