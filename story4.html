Chapter Five

The Master Mind

Now the Lone Wolf has won over 90% of the wild, leading them all by himself. Despite the fact that he controls over 90% of the wild, he is still alone because he lost his family during the last visit.

They said he was never useful to them, so they would not bow to him — that they would rather die fighting than surrender all they have to a stray wolf who doesn't have a pack he belongs to.

But one thing came to his mind when his parents made that statement, he said:
"I may not have a wolf pack, but this pack of mine is way stronger than ten times their wolf pack."
LW01d.jpg — The lone wolf who became a king
Known across the wild as
"MASTER MIND"

The lone wolf became the most popular, important, respected, feared and honoured animal in the entire wild.
